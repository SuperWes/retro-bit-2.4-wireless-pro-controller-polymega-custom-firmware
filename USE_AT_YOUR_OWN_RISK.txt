USE AT YOUR OWN RISK - EXPERIMENTAL, UNOFFICIAL FIRMWARE
=====================================================

Flashing this firmware can permanently disable (brick) your USB receiver.
Installing it on the wrong device can also disable that device. Do not proceed
unless you accept the possibility of losing the hardware and paying for its
repair or replacement.

PROJECT RECOMMENDATION
Keep using stock firmware for normal use. Do not flash your only or irreplaceable
receiver on the assumption that you can simply reinstall stock firmware later.
Hardware testing should begin with a spare receiver and an independently
verified recovery method; no such recovery has been demonstrated for this
custom image. Using the receiver only on Polymega does not reduce flashing risk.

HARDWARE TARGET
These custom images are intended ONLY for the Retro-Bit V2 USB receiver used
with the Saturn 2.4 GHz Wireless Pro controller. Verify the receiver's V2 label.
NEVER flash these receiver images onto the controller, a Saturn-port receiver,
a V1 receiver, or another hardware model or revision. If you cannot positively
identify the hardware, do not flash it.

WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN VALIDATED
One user reported successful installation and C/Z toggle operation with the
custom image on a V2 USB receiver and Polymega on November 16, 2026. This is a
limited user report, not a broad compatibility or recovery test. Automated
checks cover reproducible builds, file integrity, selected 8051 instructions
and simulated USB reports. Passing checks, matching hashes and
preserving boot-region bytes do NOT establish that flashing is safe or that
the device will boot, pair, or function correctly afterward. The earlier
NiGHTS analog test used stock firmware; the later custom-firmware report did
not separately itemize analog, every button, or receiver power-cycle behavior.

RECOVERY IS NOT GUARANTEED
Keep the official stock firmware and vendor update instructions available.
Their availability does not guarantee that a damaged receiver can enter update
mode or accept a restoration image. No recovery or unbricking procedure has been
verified for this custom firmware. Interrupted updates and incorrect images can
leave hardware unusable. The vendor's instructions describe its official update;
they do not certify or approve this project's modified firmware.
