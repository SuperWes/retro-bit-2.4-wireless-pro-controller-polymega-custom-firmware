USE AT YOUR OWN RISK - UNOFFICIAL CUSTOM FIRMWARE
===============================================

Flashing this firmware can permanently disable (brick) your USB receiver.
Installing it on the wrong device can also disable that device. Do not proceed
unless you accept the possibility of losing the hardware and paying for its
repair or replacement.

BEFORE FLASHING
Do not flash your only or irreplaceable receiver on the assumption that you
can simply reinstall stock firmware later. No recovery after a failed flash
has been demonstrated for this custom image. Using the receiver only on
Polymega does not reduce flashing risk.

HARDWARE TARGET
These custom images are intended ONLY for the Retro-Bit V2 USB receiver used
with the Saturn 2.4 GHz Wireless Pro controller. Verify the receiver's V2 label.
NEVER flash these receiver images onto the controller, a Saturn-port receiver,
a V1 receiver, or another hardware model or revision. If you cannot positively
identify the hardware, do not flash it.

WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN VALIDATED
On November 16, 2026, the author reported testing both Street Fighter and SEGA
layouts on their V2 USB receiver with Polymega and said both work great.
This is a successful user report on one setup, not a guarantee for every
receiver, host, or game and not a recovery test.

Automated checks cover reproducible builds, file integrity, selected 8051
instructions and simulated USB reports. Passing checks, matching hashes and
preserving boot-region bytes do NOT establish that every update will be safe
or that a receiver can be recovered. The earlier NiGHTS analog test used stock
firmware; the custom-firmware reports did not separately itemize analog,
every individual button, or receiver power-cycle behavior.

RECOVERY IS NOT GUARANTEED
Keep the official stock firmware and vendor update instructions available.
Their availability does not guarantee that a damaged receiver can enter update
mode or accept a restoration image. No recovery or unbricking procedure has been
verified for this custom firmware. Interrupted updates and incorrect images can
leave hardware unusable. The vendor's instructions describe its official update;
they do not certify or approve this project's modified firmware.
